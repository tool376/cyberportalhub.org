Image Metadata (Exif) Reader

Extract camera model, date/time and GPS location embedded in a photo — useful for verifying when and where an image was taken.

Nothing leaves this device. The image is read entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop a photo here, or click to choose (JPEG works best — most edited/re-saved images have Exif data stripped)

Static utility tool. Absence of metadata does not confirm an image is unedited — many platforms and apps strip Exif data automatically on upload or share. Part of cyberportalhub.org.
